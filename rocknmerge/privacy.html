Rock'n'Merge Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

In short

  • Rock'n'Merge asks for no account, no name and no email address.
  • Your games, scores and settings stay on your phone: we never receive them.
  • The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. They are personalized only if you agree, and you can change your mind at any time in the game settings.
  • The “Remove ads” purchase is paid through Google Play: we never see your payment details.

1. Who we are

Rock'n'Merge is a mobile game published by Thierry Thevenoud, Forcalqueiret, France (“we”). We are the data controller for the processing described on this page.

For any question about your data: contact.clickweb@gmail.com.

2. Data that stays on your phone

So that you can get back to your game and preferences, the game stores only on your device:

  • the game in progress and the last moves played (for “undo” and “continue”);
  • your best score and your daily challenge results;
  • your settings (music, sounds, vibration, volumes, language);
  • whether you bought “Remove ads”, and your answer to the consent form;
  • a game counter, used to space out ads between games.

This data is never sent to us. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app data (Android Settings › Apps › Rock'n'Merge › Storage) or by uninstalling the game.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free thanks to advertising: a banner at the bottom of the screen, a full-screen ad from time to time between two games (never during a game), and ads you choose to watch in exchange for a helper (undoing moves, continuing after a loss, breaking a stone).

These ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited). To show ads, measure their display and prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • your device’s advertising ID;
  • your IP address and an approximate location derived from it;
  • technical information about the device (model, Android version, language);
  • your interactions with the ads (impressions, clicks).

The app also uses Android’s advertising APIs (Privacy Sandbox: topics and ad measurement), included by Google’s SDK. They run on your device and can be managed in Android Settings › Security and privacy › Ads privacy.

Your consent

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, a form asks you, on first launch, whether you accept ad personalization.

  • If you accept: ads may be tailored to your interests.
  • If you refuse: you will see non-personalized ads, chosen based on context. Google may still process some data to show them, limit how often they repeat and prevent fraud.

You can change your mind at any time: game settings (gear icon) › “Manage privacy preferences”. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Google › Ads).

Learn more: how Google uses data from partner apps and Google’s privacy policy.

4. The “Remove ads” purchase

This one-time purchase removes the banner and the ads between games; the ads you choose to watch in exchange for a helper remain available.

  • Payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We never receive your name, card or email address.
  • To verify the purchase and let you get it back (“Restore purchases”), we use the RevenueCat service (RevenueCat, Inc.). It receives the transaction details from Google Play (product, date, price, country, purchase token) linked to an anonymous identifier generated by the app, as well as technical information about the device. RevenueCat privacy policy.

5. What we don’t do

  • We don’t ask for an account, your identity, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • We currently use no analytics tool. If this changes, this page will be updated beforehand.
  • We don’t sell any data.

7. Transfers outside the European Union

Google and RevenueCat may process data in the United States. These transfers are covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and/or the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

8. Children

Rock'n'Merge is not specifically intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data about them. If you believe a child has sent us data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, object to, restrict and port your data, and to withdraw your consent at any time.

  • For local data: clear the app data or uninstall the app.
  • For advertising: “Manage privacy preferences” in the game settings, and Android’s ad settings.
  • For any other request: contact.clickweb@gmail.com. We reply within one month.

You may also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority, for example the CNIL in France.

10. Permissions requested by the app

  • Internet and network state: loading ads and verifying the purchase.
  • Vibration: haptic feedback on merges (can be turned off in settings).
  • Advertising ID and Android advertising APIs: used by Google’s AdMob SDK (see section 3).
  • Wake lock and foreground service: used internally by Google’s SDKs for technical tasks.

11. Changes

If we change this policy (for example by adding an analytics tool), we will update this page and the date at the top. In case of a significant change, we will let you know in the game.
